Effective: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KENZ MARKET NOKOS ("we", "us", or "our") may collect, use, store, and disclose information in connection with our sales channels and customer support. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate the service.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Order information: product or country requested, order reference, delivery status, and purchase history.
  • Contact information: Discord username or user ID, messaging handle, and information you choose to send to support.
  • Payment information: payment status, transaction reference, amount, and payment confirmation. We do not intend to collect or store full payment-card credentials.
  • Service information: virtual number details necessary to fulfill an order, provider response, and technical or error logs.
  • Discord application data: if you interact with our Discord bot, it may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, interaction data, and command inputs needed to respond. Only include this paragraph if your bot actually processes these data.

2. Verification Codes and Sensitive Information

Do not send passwords, personal account credentials, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive information to our support team. If a verification message or code is processed as part of an order, access should be limited to the people and systems needed to complete that order. We should not retain verification codes longer than operationally necessary. Our actual handling of codes must match this policy and applicable law.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to confirm and fulfill orders, provide customer support, verify payment status, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service security, troubleshoot technical issues, keep appropriate business records, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases and Purpose Limitation

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to perform a requested transaction, pursue legitimate business interests such as security and fraud prevention, comply with legal obligations, or with your consent where required. We do not use customer information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the service, such as hosting, payment, communication, or number-supply providers, only as reasonably necessary for their services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including order support, dispute resolution, security, and legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods vary by data type. Verification codes and temporary technical data should be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.
